  - [x] 23b. The 3-point arc tool (key A, the Arc button after Circle): the start, the end, then a point on the arc, all snapped; ends on existing points are shared, the centre shares a point only if one is exactly there, the third click is not kept; one undo step "Draw arc". `arcThrough` in `sketch-tool.ts` puts the centre at m + d⊥·k on the line midway between the ends (k = (|p − m|² − |d|²/4) / (2·(d × (p − a))), no square root) and swaps the ends when the third click is on the left, so the arc runs counter-clockwise through it. Clicks that make no bend are refused with a note when the cross product is within a rounding bound (not a tolerance: on a 0.1 mm grid three nodes on one line leave a cross product of about 1e-17), and an end on the start too. The preview is a line to the pointer, then the arc through it. Notes from step 21 for later steps:
- Points join only when their snapped coordinates are equal (point snapping comes with constraints, 27). No AI commands for sketching or extruding yet (the AI workstream's call).
- A sketch can't be opened again for editing once finished, so the extrude's live link to its sketch (the part rebuilds after a sketch edit, `applySketchEdit`) is reachable in the app only from step 33 (edit any feature); it is tested in `extrude.test.ts` and `extrude-sketch.journey.ts`.
- Extruding a sketch on XZ or YZ is refused with a message until sketches on other planes exist (24): the kernel extrudes along +Z only. Only positive distances, one direction (no symmetric or two-sided yet).
- A preset after an extrude takes the height box's value as its height (the boxes are shared), and takes new counter ids.

Notes from step 23 for later steps:
- An arc's centre is a point of its own: moving an end (`movePoint`) leaves the centre off the midway line until constraints (27) keep them together, and the kernel then names the arc and how far off it is. A nearly whole arc on a short chord is the sensitive case: its far side moves by as much as the centre is off the midway line, which is why the rule is on that distance and not on the ends' distances from the centre (those differ by only about off × chord / radius).
- The profile (`ops::profile`) rebuilds an arc's radius and centre from its bulge; for a very shallow arc those carry the sagitta's rounding (relative error about rounding / sagitta) while the curve itself stays within rounding. So right at the radius limit (MAX_SIZE) a shallow arc `check_arcs` accepts may still be refused by the profile check, as "not a valid outline", never an error.

Notes from step 22 for later steps:
- A circle together with any other curve is "the sketch makes n separate loops" until regions (26). The circle tool keeps only the centre as a point; the click on the circle sets the radius (√(dx² + dy²) with Math.sqrt, not Math.hypot, which V8 rounds a unit off for (10, 30)).
- The viewport draws a circle as `CIRCLE_SEGMENTS` = 64 straight segments (`circleSegments`, drawing only; the sketch and the kernel keep the exact circle), so its `sketchLines` count is 64 per circle.
