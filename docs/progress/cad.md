Notes from step 23 for later steps:
- The tangent arc (23c, key T, `tangentArc` and `leaving` in sketch-tool.ts) leaves a free end (a point where exactly one line or arc ends; a corner has two ways out, a centre none) along that curve, and is tangent only by construction: its centre is put on the normal at the start, but nothing keeps it tangent after a move until the tangent constraint (30). It chains like the line tool (owner asked on 2026-10-17 whether Fusion does the same; the default chains), and switching between L and T keeps the chain's last point (`switchTool`), so a slot is four clicks after the first. A click straight on or behind is refused by 23b's rounding bound (`bend`, shared), checked even after an arc whose rounded centre sets the way out.
- An arc too shallow for the kernel's MAX_SIZE radius (a far click a hair off straight on a fine grid) is drawn and then named by the kernel's outline check; the tools don't know the kernel's limits.
- An arc's centre is a point of its own: moving an end (`movePoint`) leaves the centre off the midway line until constraints (29) keep them together, and the kernel then names the arc and how far off it is. A nearly whole arc on a short chord is the sensitive case: its far side moves by as much as the centre is off the midway line, which is why the rule is on that distance and not on the ends' distances from the centre (those differ by only about off × chord / radius).
- The profile (`ops::profile`) rebuilds an arc's radius and centre from its bulge; for a very shallow arc those carry the sagitta's rounding (relative error about rounding / sagitta) while the curve itself stays within rounding. So right at the radius limit (MAX_SIZE) a shallow arc `check_arcs` accepts may still be refused by the profile check, as "not a valid outline", never an error.
