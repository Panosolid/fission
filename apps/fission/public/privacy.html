Privacy and terms

Last changed 22 October 2026. Every change to this page is public in the project's history.

Privacy

The short version: Panosolid has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cookies and no trackers. Your designs stay in your browser on your device. The people who make Panosolid never receive them, or anything else about you.

What is kept, and where

  • Your projects, and the copy kept as you work, are saved in this browser's own storage on this device. They are not sent anywhere and not synced to other devices. Use Save to keep a copy as a file.
  • Settings, including the AI assistant's settings and any AI key you enter, are kept in this browser's storage on this device.
  • If you download the in-browser AI model, its files (about 0.9 GB) are kept in this browser's cache.

What leaves your device

  • Loading the site. Panosolid is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees your IP address and browser details when you load it, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The AI assistant. Nothing is sent to an online AI service unless you choose one and give it your own key. When you open the assistant, an AI that runs on your own device may be chosen for you; that one keeps everything on your device.
    • With Anthropic, OpenAI or another address you type, your request and a short summary of your project (the names and sizes of its features and any recent errors, not the shapes themselves) go straight from your browser to that provider, using your own key. Before the first request to each address, Panosolid shows exactly what will be sent and waits for you. That provider's own privacy policy and terms then apply, and you pay them directly. Panosolid never sees your requests or your key.
    • With Ollama on your own computer, nothing leaves your computer. If you point it at another computer's address, it is treated like any other provider and asks you first.
    • The in-browser model is downloaded once from Hugging Face and GitHub, and after that runs on your device: your requests stay there.
  • Files you save or export (projects, STL files and the like) are downloaded to your device. Where they go next is up to you.

Children and students

Panosolid asks for no personal information, so anyone can use it. The hosted AI providers set their own minimum ages (many require you to be 18, some allow 13 with a parent's permission). If you are under a provider's age, don't use a key for it; Ollama and the in-browser model need no account.

Deleting your data

Everything Panosolid keeps is in your browser, so you control it:

  • To remove an AI key, open the assistant panel and choose Remove key.
  • To delete everything (projects, settings, keys and any downloaded model), clear the site data for panosolid.github.io in your browser's settings. This can't be undone, so save any project you want to keep to a file first.
  • There is no other copy anywhere for us to delete. For anything you sent to an AI provider, ask that provider.
