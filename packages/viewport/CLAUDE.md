# Viewport

Contents of `packages/viewport`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the one Three.js viewport every workspace draws into (`Viewer`, implements `SharedViewport`: one scene seen through one or more `ViewportView`s, each its own camera, for split view; `paneViewports` gives each workspace on screen a `SharedViewport` over its own view), the camera re-framing rule, and the image sheet with its top-down 2D mode (`image-sheet.ts`, G1b; each view shows its own image on a layer only its camera sees; `showImage(null)` is the same 2D view with no sheet, for a workspace with nothing open).
- sketches (step 21c): `showSketches` draws sketch lines over the body (the edited one with its grid, points and rubber band; the ground grid hides meanwhile), `showPlanes` the origin planes to pick (`ViewportPick.plane`), `onSketchPointer` gets clicks and hovers on the edited sketch's plane instead of picking; the maths (plane frame, ray meets plane, grid lines, `pixelSize`: mm per screen pixel at the pointer, sent as `SketchPointer.pixel` for snapping) in `sketch-view.ts`; a drawing's `editing.snap` marks are drawn in green over everything, its label in `data-sketch-snap`.
- seeing what you draw (sketch feel SF2): while a sketch is edited the body is see-through (`BODY_FADE`, `data-body-faded`), the model axes give way to the sketch's red x and green y axes, and the grid follows the main view's camera (`sketchGrid`, `gridSpacing`, `minorStrength` in `sketch-view.ts`: powers of ten at least 8 px apart, or the snap grid's multiples when `editing.gridSnap`; redrawn in `Viewer.frame` when the camera moves; `data-sketch-grid`, `data-sketch-axes`); `lookAt(plane)` puts a view's camera square onto a plane (`planeViewBasis` in `views.ts`, framing from `lookAtFraming` in `framing.ts`).
- moving the camera by hand (sketch feel SF3a, the owner's choice of bindings): `navigation.ts` holds the maths (`zoomAt`: camera and target scale about the focus-plane point under the cursor; `panBy`: the grabbed point follows the pointer, `focusPixel` mm a pixel; `orbitBy`: Z-up spherical turn, kept `POLE_EPS` off the poles; `wheelFactor`, `dragZoomFactor`, `clampZoom`, `cubeDragAngles`; `dragAction`: middle pans, Shift+middle orbits, the right button is kept for a menu, left follows the `NavMode`); `ViewportView.navigate()` wires the wheel (Ctrl = trackpad pinch), middle-drag and Pan/Zoom-mode left-drag ahead of OrbitControls (capture), which keeps orbiting and touch; dragging the view cube orbits (`viewcube.ts`). `nav-bar.ts`: each view's navigation bar (`.view-controls`, styles in packages/ui `styles/view-controls.css`): Orbit, Pan, Zoom (`data-nav`, `aria-pressed`; the view's `data-nav`), Fit, Look at (on only while a sketch is edited), Home, Top, Front, Right (`data-view`). `cameraPose()` is read by the e2e test through `fissionTest.camera`.
