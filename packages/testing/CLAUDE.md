- the Workspace API boundary test, the "zero exports" journeys (`journeys/*.journey.ts`, on the real kernel through `src/node-kernel.ts`; `JOURNEYS`/`ROUND_TRIPS` list the ones waiting), the magic-moment journeys (`src/magic.ts`: `MAGIC_JOURNEYS` with click and time limits, two ready: Design's shape to STL and sketch to STL (25c4), `judge`, `reportLine`; walked in the browser by `apps/fission/e2e/journeys.mjs`; compared with `tests/baseline.json` "journeys" by `parseBaselines`, `compareToBaseline`, `baselineLine`), and DRAFT harness interfaces.
- `journeys/sketch-kernel.journey.ts`: the sketch ops on the real kernel (step 21a): hand-worked snaps and a 3-4-5 triangle, then 5 seeds × 40 snapped star polygons drawn as shuffled, reversed lines, each closing into the loop drawn with volume = shoelace area × height, and opening again when one end moves a grid step; a saved sketch's outline through a live link (opened from its saved text), 1200 mm² then 1600 mm² after a corner moves, and refused with the kernel's reason once a side is deleted (step 21b); 5 seeds × 40 snapped polygons with sides bowed out as arcs, shuffled, closing into the loop drawn with volume = (shoelace area + each arc's segment r²(φ − sin φ)/2) × height, refused once an arc's centre moves 2 × LINEAR off its midway line (step 23a).
- `journeys/free-placement.journey.ts`: free placement on the real kernel (sketch feel SF1): a click rounded by `place_freely` and the grid by hand, then 5 seeds × 40 free star polygons (pixels 1 µm to 1 mm) drawn through `rounding`, `snapOnSketch` and `clickAt`, each click within half a spacing of the pointer on a whole number of spacings (model: the pixel's printed exponent) unless it snapped, closed by a click near the first point, extruded to shoelace area × height, opened by moving one end; 3 seeds × 100 free 3-point arcs accepted by the kernel or refused with a note.
- `journeys/timeline-verbs.journey.ts` (step 25c1): Design's verbs and hand-overs on timelines on the real kernel: a box and an extruded rectangle as one STL (24 triangles, 36,000 mm³ by hand), a rectangle sketch's Copy as SVG from its row, and 200 seeded timelines (`timelineEdits` from `src/project-fuzz.ts`) whose verbs, toolbar STL, copies and links agree with a model of what is shown (`buildModel` less the kernel's failures).
