# Testing

Contents of `packages/testing`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the Workspace API boundary test, the "zero exports" journeys (`journeys/*.journey.ts`, on the real kernel through `src/node-kernel.ts`; `JOURNEYS`/`ROUND_TRIPS` list the ones waiting), the magic-moment journeys (`src/magic.ts`: `MAGIC_JOURNEYS` with click and time limits, `judge`, `reportLine`; walked in the browser by `apps/fission/e2e/journeys.mjs`; compared with `tests/baseline.json` "journeys" by `parseBaselines`, `compareToBaseline`, `baselineLine`), and DRAFT harness interfaces.
- `journeys/sketch-kernel.journey.ts`: the sketch ops on the real kernel (step 21a): hand-worked snaps and a 3-4-5 triangle, then 5 seeds × 40 snapped star polygons drawn as shuffled, reversed lines, each closing into the loop drawn with volume = shoelace area × height, and opening again when one end moves a grid step; a saved sketch's outline through a live link (opened from its saved text), 1200 mm² then 1600 mm² after a corner moves, and refused with the kernel's reason once a side is deleted (step 21b); 5 seeds × 40 snapped polygons with sides bowed out as arcs, shuffled, closing into the loop drawn with volume = (shoelace area + each arc's segment r²(φ − sin φ)/2) × height, refused once an arc's centre moves 2 × LINEAR off its midway line (step 23a).
- `journeys/extrude-sketch.journey.ts`: extruding a sketch on the real kernel (step 21d): hand-worked volumes and areas (a 40 × 30 rectangle, a corner moved, a 3-4-5 triangle), an open sketch refused with the kernel's reason, and 5 seeds × 8 sessions of sketch edits, extrudes, distances and presets against a corners-only model (volume = shoelace area × distance); rectangles (22a); circles (22b: radius 5 by hand, 50π mm³ and 70π mm², two loops refused; radius at 0.9× and 1.1× of 0.001 and 500000 mm; 5 seeds × 40 circles drawn with the circle tool from two snapped clicks, πr²d; the tool's hand-worked 1000π mm³ and 400π mm², 22c); arcs (23a: a pie slice by hand, 100π mm³ and 70π + 80 mm², the arc drawn the other way the other three quarters, an end moved 1 µm refused; 3 seeds × 30 half discs from two snapped clicks, πr²d/2).
- `test/history-fuzz.test.ts`: seeded edit/undo/redo sessions on a real project against a two-stack model (step 20a), with sketch edits (draw, chain, delete, move a point: step 21b; circles, some round a point already there: 22b; arcs, some from a point already there: 23a) checked against lines, circles and arcs kept as coordinates, new line ids never seen in any state undo can reach, and extrudes with typed distances (step 21d: the part kept as feature id and size, new feature ids never seen in a reachable state).
- `src/project-fuzz.ts`: fuzzing the project loader (`projectFromJson` with every workspace's reader; valid inputs include Design data with sketches (lines, circles and arcs), with a part extruding one (21d), and Design version 1 data, whose upgrade is the one change a save may make) against a model of the outer layout plus open/show/save/reopen checks, with shrinking; `test/project-fuzz.test.ts` runs it quickly (3 seeds × 1,500 cases), replays `test/regressions/project-fuzz/*.json` and shows each check failing; `fuzz/project-loader.ts` (`npm run fuzz -w @fission/testing`, env `FISSION_FUZZ_SECONDS`, `FISSION_SEED` decimal, `FISSION_FUZZ_OUT`) is the long run the nightly `fuzz.yml` uses.
- `baseline/app.ts`: the app's speed and memory for `tests/baseline.json` (opening and saving a big project, 200 edits undone, kernel calls through the WASM build): JavaScript heap kept after GC, the kernel's WebAssembly memory, time relative to a fixed workload; run by `.github/scripts/baseline.py` (CI job `baseline`).
- `src/ai-eval.ts`: the AI evaluation suite (`EVAL_CASES`, `runEval`, `successRates`; each result says whether the answer was usable first try); `scripts/ai-eval-ollama.ts` scores real models through Ollama for the A10 check, run by `.github/workflows/ai-eval.yml` (AI3).
- `src/timeline-model.ts`: a model of the timeline (steps 24-25) written apart from Design, the oracle for the random timeline sessions: items with counter ids, suppressed flags, the rollback marker and whole-document undo; sketches as grid polygons (exact integer validity), circles and slots; `rebuild` gives each item's status or failure cause and each body's volume and faces by name (`FaceName`: feature, role, curve) with area and centroid; `MODEL_FAULTS` are planted bugs for the harness's own tests. Hand-worked in `test/timeline-model.test.ts`.
- `src/timeline-session.ts`: seeded timeline sessions (`randomSession`, `runSession`) run on the model and a `TimelineSubject` (the model itself until 24d, then Design's timeline), checking outcome, fresh ids, rebuild determinism and from scratch, the model's rebuild, unique names, edit locality, held references and save/open; `shrinkSession` cuts a failure to a 1-minimal list. `test/timeline-session.test.ts`: 12 seeds × 150 steps reach every outcome, each planted bug caught by its check, the comparer at 0.9×/1.1× of `MATCH_REL`, saved `test/regressions/timeline/*.json` replayed. `journeys/timeline.journey.ts`: the real-kernel checks, todo until their substeps.
- `src/sketch-extrude-session.ts`: seeded sketch-and-extrude sessions (`runSession`, `sessionSeed`): a simulated user draws with Design's tools (line chains, 3-point arcs, rectangles, circles; on a grid or free), extrudes, types distances, moves corners, deletes and redraws sides, swaps in a preset and reopens; each step's part checked against the model here (`measureShape`: shoelace plus each arc's segment from its sagitta; `clearlySimple`, so the model only predicts loops it is sure of), to `MATCH_REL`; `FAULTS` are planted bugs. `journeys/sketch-extrude-sessions.journey.ts`: hand-worked shape by the tools, the comparer at 0.9×/1.1×, the simple-loop check's refusals, `FISSION_SESSIONS` × `FISSION_SESSION_STEPS` sessions (40 × 30), each fault caught, `test/regressions/sketch-extrude/*.json` (`{seed, steps}`) replayed. `fuzz/sketch-extrude.ts` (`npm run sessions -w @fission/testing`, after the WASM build; `FISSION_FUZZ_SECONDS`, `FISSION_SEED`, `FISSION_FUZZ_OUT` with `oracle.jsonl` for `prototypes/oracle/sketch_extrude.py`) is the nightly run in `fuzz.yml`.
