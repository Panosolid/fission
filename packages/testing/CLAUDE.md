# Testing

Contents of `packages/testing`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the Workspace API boundary test, the "zero exports" journeys (`journeys/*.journey.ts`, on the real kernel through `src/node-kernel.ts`; `JOURNEYS`/`ROUND_TRIPS` list the ones waiting), the magic-moment journeys (`src/magic.ts`: `MAGIC_JOURNEYS` with click and time limits, two ready: Design's shape to STL and sketch to STL (25c4), `judge`, `reportLine`; walked in the browser by `apps/fission/e2e/journeys.mjs`; compared with `tests/baseline.json` "journeys" by `parseBaselines`, `compareToBaseline`, `baselineLine`), and DRAFT harness interfaces.
- `journeys/sketch-kernel.journey.ts`: the sketch ops on the real kernel (step 21a): hand-worked snaps and a 3-4-5 triangle, then 5 seeds × 40 snapped star polygons drawn as shuffled, reversed lines, each closing into the loop drawn with volume = shoelace area × height, and opening again when one end moves a grid step; a saved sketch's outline through a live link (opened from its saved text), 1200 mm² then 1600 mm² after a corner moves, and refused with the kernel's reason once a side is deleted (step 21b); 5 seeds × 40 snapped polygons with sides bowed out as arcs, shuffled, closing into the loop drawn with volume = (shoelace area + each arc's segment r²(φ − sin φ)/2) × height, refused once an arc's centre moves 2 × LINEAR off its midway line (step 23a).
- `journeys/free-placement.journey.ts`: free placement on the real kernel (sketch feel SF1): a click rounded by `place_freely` and the grid by hand, then 5 seeds × 40 free star polygons (pixels 1 µm to 1 mm) drawn through `rounding`, `snapOnSketch` and `clickAt`, each click within half a spacing of the pointer on a whole number of spacings (model: the pixel's printed exponent) unless it snapped, closed by a click near the first point, extruded to shoelace area × height, opened by moving one end; 3 seeds × 100 free 3-point arcs accepted by the kernel or refused with a note.
- `journeys/timeline-verbs.journey.ts` (step 25c1): Design's verbs and hand-overs on timelines on the real kernel: a box and an extruded rectangle as one STL (24 triangles, 36,000 mm³ by hand), a rectangle sketch's Copy as SVG from its row, and 200 seeded timelines (`timelineEdits` from `src/project-fuzz.ts`) whose verbs, toolbar STL, copies and links agree with a model of what is shown (`buildModel` less the kernel's failures).
- `journeys/extrude-sketch.journey.ts`: extruding a sketch on the real kernel (step 21d): hand-worked volumes and areas (a 40 × 30 rectangle, a corner moved, a 3-4-5 triangle), an open sketch refused with the kernel's reason, and 5 seeds × 8 sessions of sketch edits, extrudes, distances and presets against a corners-only model (volume = shoelace area × distance); rectangles (22a); circles (22b: radius 5 by hand, 50π mm³ and 70π mm², two loops refused; radius at 0.9× and 1.1× of 0.001 and 500000 mm; 5 seeds × 40 circles drawn with the circle tool from two snapped clicks, πr²d; the tool's hand-worked 1000π mm³ and 400π mm², 22c); arcs (23a: a pie slice by hand, 100π mm³ and 70π + 80 mm², the arc drawn the other way the other three quarters, an end moved 1 µm refused; 3 seeds × 30 half discs from two snapped clicks, πr²d/2).
- `test/history-fuzz.test.ts`: seeded edit/undo/redo sessions on a real project against a two-stack model (step 20a), with sketch edits (draw, chain, delete, move a point: step 21b; circles, some round a point already there: 22b; arcs, some from a point already there: 23a) checked against lines, circles and arcs kept as coordinates, new line ids never seen in any state undo can reach, and extrudes with typed distances (step 21d: the part kept as feature id and size, new feature ids never seen in a reachable state); timeline edits (24e3: delete, move with both refusals, suppress, the marker) against a timeline model of items, marker and the part as the last solid item that builds; an edit that builds nothing must make `lastFeature` refuse and is not made. Step 25b2: Edit feature (`editFeature`) on any solid item as one never-merged step, and several sketches, drawn into only when the item menu would open them (Edit Sketch), sometimes an earlier one.
- `src/project-fuzz.ts`: fuzzing the project loader (`projectFromJson` with every workspace's reader; valid inputs include Design data with sketches (lines, circles and arcs), with a part extruding one (21d), with several solid features, some suppressed, and a marker (24d1), timelines left by Design's own edits (25b1: `timelineEdits`, with kept shapes, suppressed, rolled-back and failing items, reorders and Edit feature), and Design version 1 and 2 data, whose upgrades are the one change a save may make) against a model of the outer layout plus open/show/save/reopen checks and a build model (`buildModel`) that Design's `buildingItems`, `builds` and `partFeature` must agree with on every file that opens (`BuildRules` lets a test plant a bug), with shrinking; `test/project-fuzz.test.ts` runs it quickly (3 seeds × 1,500 cases), replays `test/regressions/project-fuzz/*.json` and shows each check failing; `fuzz/project-loader.ts` (`npm run fuzz -w @fission/testing`, env `FISSION_FUZZ_SECONDS`, `FISSION_SEED` decimal, `FISSION_FUZZ_OUT`) is the long run the nightly `fuzz.yml` uses.
- `test/ai-view.test.ts` (step 25c3): what the AI is told about 300 seeded timelines (`timelineEdits`) against `buildModel`, each item's state and body number and the marker, and the set_part it says changes the part or adds a body done with `documentFor`: in place, or one item where it said.
- `baseline/app.ts`: the app's speed and memory for `tests/baseline.json` (opening and saving a big project, 200 edits undone, kernel calls through the WASM build): JavaScript heap kept after GC, the kernel's WebAssembly memory, time relative to a fixed workload; and one sketch edit on a 20-feature part (`baseline/rebuild.ts`, step 24d3c: the starting box and 20 extrudes with arcs, the first sketch's corner moved, rebuilt and drawn as `workspace.ts` does), timed and counted exactly (`kernel_calls`, `body_builds`), with planted bugs `REBUILD_FAULTS` (`FISSION_BASELINE_FAULT`); run by `.github/scripts/baseline.py` (CI job `baseline`). `journeys/rebuild-baseline.journey.ts` hand-works that edit's volumes and counts and checks each planted bug keeps the shapes but changes the counts.
- `src/ai-eval.ts`: the AI evaluation suite (`EVAL_CASES`, `runEval`, `successRates`; each result says whether the answer was usable first try) and `HELD_OUT_CASES` (AI4: never used while writing the prompt, never edited to fit one; add new cases instead); `matches` judges the part the answer leaves (`partAfter`), `commandsMatch` compares command by command (AI5's training checks); `scripts/ai-eval-ollama.ts` scores both sets separately on real models through Ollama for the A10 check, run by `.github/workflows/ai-eval.yml` (AI3).
- `src/outline-model.ts`: a test-only model of the sketch outline rules (`modelOutline`: empty, loose end, branch, separate loops, not a profile, closed with its shoelace area, a lone circle; exact on whole-number coordinates; `openWords` the kernel's words; `compareOutline` checks a kernel answer against it); hand-worked in `test/outline-model.test.ts`.
- `journeys/sketch-mixed.journey.ts`: the tools mixed in one sketch on the real kernel (testing catch-up TC1): hand-worked mixes (a rectangle and a circle, a chain off a rectangle's corner, a house from a rectangle with its top deleted and a roof of lines, straight-on and turned-back corners), 5 seeds × 60 random sessions of all three tools with Escapes and deletes (every answer kind reached at least 10 times), and 5 seeds × 30 closed two-tool outlines extruded to area × 10 mm, each answer checked against `src/outline-model.ts`.
- `src/timeline-model.ts`: a model of the timeline (steps 24-25) written apart from Design, the oracle for the random timeline sessions: items with counter ids, suppressed flags, the rollback marker and whole-document undo; sketches as grid polygons (exact integer validity), circles and slots; `rebuild` gives each item's status or failure cause (an extrude whose sketch is lost built from its `kept` shape with a `warning` instead, the owner's rule, kept by `settle` after every edit: 24d2) and each body's volume, faces by name (`FaceName`: feature, role, curve) with area and centroid, and edges by the two faces they join (`EdgeName`, `edgeKey`, step 24c) with closed-form length and centroid; `bodyKeys` and `buildBounds` (step 24e) say what each body is built from and how many bodies an edit must and may build; `MODEL_FAULTS` are planted bugs for the harness's own tests. Hand-worked in `test/timeline-model.test.ts`.
- `src/timeline-session.ts`: seeded timeline sessions (`randomSession`, `runSession`, async: a subject may answer with promises, start with a part (`initial`), refuse to open a document with nothing built, and measure edges on the mesh, `EdgeClose`) run on the model and a `TimelineSubject` (the model itself, and Design's timeline from 24d2), checking outcome, fresh ids, rebuild determinism and from scratch, the model's rebuild, unique names, edit locality, held references, save/open, and (24e, when the subject counts them with `builds`) that each step's rebuild builds only what changed; `shrinkSession` cuts a failure to a 1-minimal list. `test/timeline-session.test.ts`: 12 seeds × 150 steps reach every outcome, each planted bug caught by its check, the comparer at 0.9×/1.1× of `MATCH_REL`, saved `test/regressions/timeline/*.json` replayed. `journeys/timeline.journey.ts`: the real-kernel checks, todo until their substeps; 24b's are live: each face of a sketch drawn shuffled and backwards, extruded on the real kernel and named through `face_roles` and the outline's `pieces` (`named`), against the model's names, areas and centroids (hand-worked cases, presets, 5 seeds × 40 random sketches); 24c's: every edge named through Design's `nameBody` and measured from the mesh's edge lines against the model (a half disc's two uprights and its arc edge by hand, the comparer at 0.9×/1.1×, planted bugs), and a selected face keeping its name through a delete-and-redraw that renumbers the faces.
- `src/naming-model.ts` (step 25a1): a model of a sketch on a face when earlier features change, written apart from Design, the oracle for 25a, 26 (sketch on a face) and booleans' cut (44/48): an outline on XY extruded either way (`extrude`), a circle on one of its caps or (26a) sides (`face-sketch`, holding the face by name, a side by the outline line it was swept from; its frame from `drawnFrame`, the nearest origin plane turned onto the face so coordinates read as the world's, turned with the face by `frameNow`; its side the one it was drawn from), a cut into that body (`cut`, `target` fixed when made) or a new body (`face-extrude`); `placeOn` (one face with the name: follow it; none or two: hold the last placement with "plane-lost" or "plane-ambiguous", never guess), `planDelete` (asks when anything is built from it, takes the cuts into its body), refused drags (`namingDeps`); `suppress` greys everything built on the feature with no warning (26a); a feature from a sketch whose plane is lost says "source-lost"; every solid's volume and centroid; `discPolygonArea`, and `discMeetsPolygon`, `discInsidePolygon`, `pointInPolygon` exact in integers; `clearCut` (`CLEAR_MM`) keeps random cuts where the model is sure; `NAMING_FAULTS` planted bugs.
