# Design workspace

Contents of `workspaces/design`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the CAD workspace (`workspace.ts`, which names each undo step: "Change width" merged per size box, "Change shape to …"; a mount by Undo or Redo keeps the camera and keeps whatever in the selection still resolves by name on the bodies it rebuilt (`lastScene`); `profiles.ts` ready-made outlines until the sketcher).
- `commands.ts`: Design's commands as data (`DESIGN_COMMANDS`: `set_part`, `export_stl`), used by the toolbar and the AI assistant alike; `set_part`'s depth and height are optional (a left-out size keeps the current one in `run`; `documentFor` refuses one the shape needs), tested in `test/commands.test.ts`.
- `ai-view.ts` (step 25c3): `designAIView`, what the AI assistant is told (the mounted workspace's `aiView`): the part as the set_part that makes it, the panel's sizes a set_part keeps, the timeline in build order with each item's state (the last `AI_TIMELINE_ITEMS`), the marker, and whether Accept changes the part or adds a body (`adds`: where `withPart` puts it). Hand-worked in `test/ai-view.test.ts`; seeded timelines against the build model and `documentFor` in packages/testing `test/ai-view.test.ts`.
- `document.ts`: Design's data, version 3 (step 24d1; version 2 from 21b; docs/document-model.md, docs/project-format.md): `nextId`, profiles, sketches (curves: lines, circles round a point id from step 22b, arcs from a start to an end point id counter-clockwise round a centre point id from step 23a; `curvePoints`) and the timeline with its rollback marker (`timeline.marker`: "start", an item's id, or left out for all); every solid feature is its own body; new items go in at the marker (`insertAt`, `insertItem`: just after it, the marker moving onto them); presets take counter ids (`insertPreset`, `changePreset` in place by id, `presetFeature`); `builds` (at or before the marker, not suppressed, its sketch building) and `lastFeature`, the part the app shows until 24d3: the last solid feature that builds; `withPart` (the panel, set_part) changes that part in place when it is a preset, else adds a preset; `shapeOf` any solid feature's shape and sizes, `partOf` the part's; `readDesignDocument` migrates version 1 (`migrateDesignV1`) and 2 (`migrateDesignV2`: every solid feature but the last suppressed) and checks every sketch and timeline rule; `test/timeline-data.test.ts` hand-works 24d1 on `test/files/v2-plate.fission.json`, a version 2 file saved by main; `shown` names a file's value in a message without running its own `toString`.
- `sketch.ts`: sketch edits as pure document functions (`addSketch`, `drawLine` with ends as a point id to share or new coordinates, `drawRectangle` (step 22a: four chained lines, corners on existing points shared; `pointAt`), `drawCircle` (step 22b: round a point id to share or new coordinates, radius above 0), `drawArc` (step 23a: start, end and centre each a point id or new coordinates, ids taken in that order; one undo step "Draw arc"), `deleteCurve` dropping points only it used, `movePoint`), each one undo step named in `SKETCH_STEPS`; ids from the counter, never reused; `planeOf` the origin planes' frames; `sketchOutline` asks the kernel (`sketch_outline` with `sketchCurves`, the lines, circles and arcs apart, through `askOutline`) each time, never stored; `sketchLines` is the lines alone. Sketches are `design.sketch` objects with the generic `outlineProducer` (`representations.ts`, `designObjects(doc, kernel)`), timeline rows and rows of the browser's Sketches group ("sketches/<id>").
- `sketch-tool.ts`: the sketch tools as pure functions: `SKETCH_TOOLS` (Line, Rectangle, Circle, Arc, Tangent arc with keys L, R, C, A and T, steps 22a, 22c, 23b and 23c), `startTool`, `toolBusy` (what Escape ends), `clickAt` (line: a click at a kernel-snapped point draws a chained line sharing its start, landing on an existing point ends the chain; rectangle: first corner, then the opposite one, refused with a `note` on its row or column; circle: the centre (an existing point shared), then a point on the circle, the radius `distance` between them, refused with a `note` on the centre; arc: start, end, then a point on it, through `arcThrough` (step 23b: the centre on the line midway between the ends, ends swapped so it runs counter-clockwise, `null` within a rounding bound of one line), refused with a `note` for no end or no bend; tangent arc (step 23c): a free end (`leaving`: the way out where exactly one line or arc ends), then the arc's end, through `tangentArc` (the centre on the normal at the start, k = |w|²/(2·t × w)), chaining from its own end, refused with a `note` for no free end, no end or no turn; `bend` is the rounding bound both arcs share), `switchTool` (a tool switch: the line chain and the tangent arc hand over their last point), `drawStep` (the undo step's name), `previewLine`, `previewSegments` (what a click would draw), `circleSegments` (a circle as `CIRCLE_SEGMENTS` = 64 segments, drawing only), `arcSegments` (step 23a: an arc in ⌈64 × turn / 2π⌉ segments from its start to its end exactly), `sketchDrawings` (what the viewport draws: lines, then circles, then arcs, as segments), `curveCount` (the status's "4 lines and 1 circle", "2 lines, 1 arc and 1 circle"), `ORIGIN_PLANES` (only XY offered until 26), `PLANE_VIEWS`. `sketch-toolbar.ts` (sketch feel SF3b): the sketch's own toolbar, standing in for the modelling buttons (`data-modelling`) while a sketch is edited, Extrude kept: a Create menu (`createMenuRows`, each tool with its key; arrows by `menuMove`, Escape closes only the menu), the tool buttons (`sketch-tool-<kind>`, the one in use pressed) and a big Finish Sketch at the far right (`finishSketch` in `workspace.ts`: back to modelling with the camera at the home view, also from the panel's Finish sketch); styles `src/styles/sketchbar.css`; tested in `test/sketch-toolbar.test.ts`. `sketch-panel.ts`: the panel at the top of the properties (Pick a plane, then the tool's help, `TOOL_HELP` and `SNAP_HELP`, grid, Snap to grid (`DEFAULT_GRID_SNAP` off, in `sketch-tool.ts`; `workspace.ts` keeps the choice for every sketch until reload), Look at (SF2: `viewport.lookAt` on the sketch's plane, also on picking a plane), outline status, Finish sketch); styles in `src/styles/sketch.css`. `workspace.ts` runs the mode: Create sketch button, plane pick, `applySketchEdit` (one undo step per edit), Escape ends a chain or drops a first corner, centre or arc ends, `chooseTool` (buttons, L, R, C, A and T, through `switchTool`; a click the kernel was still snapping across a tool change is dropped), `editingSketch` keeps you in the sketch, with its tool, through Undo and Redo.
- `snap.ts`: where a sketch click lands (before constraints): after the kernel rounds the pointer (`rounding`, sketch feel SF1: `place_freely` to within half a pixel with grid snap off, the default; `snap_to_grid` with it on, or when the pixel size is unknown), within `SNAP_PIXELS` (10 px, turned to mm by `SketchPointer.pixel`) the nearest sketch point or line midpoint exactly, else lined up with the nearest point's column and/or row (H/V from the chain's last point), else the grid; never one of the tool's own clicks (`ownClicks`: a chain's last point, a first corner, a centre, an arc's start and end, a tangent arc's start), never the rectangle's first corner's row or column (`snapTargets`, `snapPointer`, `snapOnSketch`); `snapMarks` (square, triangle, ring, dashed guides sized in pixels), `snapLabel` ("endpoint 10,0", read by the e2e test), `withSnap` adds them to the edited drawing. `workspace.ts` snaps every hover and click through it.
- `rebuild.ts` (step 24d2): `rebuildTimeline(doc, cache, kernel)`, every feature's result (a body handle from `BodyCache`, which builds each spec once, keeps its view and frees every body no feature holds; or suppressed, rolled back, or failed with the model's cause); the owner's rule: an extrude whose sketch is lost builds from its kept shape (`doc.kept`, `keptOf` in `document.ts`) with a `warning` naming the sketch, the cause and the curves at fault (from the kernel's `inputs` and sketch_outline's `invalid`, never its words); `settleKept(kernel, before, after)` keeps and forgets those shapes after every edit; `extrudeSpec` is the one place the rule is applied (`featureSpec` uses it, so the Extrude panel and other workspaces agree). `workspace.ts` shows the part from `rebuildTimeline` on one `BodyCache` (rebuilds in turn, freed on unmount), settles `kept` before recording each sketch click, and shows the warning (`#kept-warning`, hidden while that sketch is edited) with the extrude's and sketch's timeline rows red (`src/styles/kept.css`); `graph.ts` leaves out an input no longer in the document. Hand-worked and checked against the model on the real kernel in packages/testing (`journeys/timeline.journey.ts`, `src/timeline-design.ts`); the data's refusals in `test/kept.test.ts`.
- Step 26c, sketch on a face: `face-sketch.ts` (the frame maths, the naming model's rule 8: `drawnFrame`, `drawnOnFace` (null on a curved face, Q5), `frameNow`, `turned`, `squared` (every frame squared in doubles before the kernel sees it), face plane names, `planeWords`); `sketch.ts` `addFaceSketch` (the face's name as the item's `plane`, `Sketch.face` the frame as drawn and its side) and `sketchPlane`/`planeFor` (where a sketch is drawn: an origin plane, or the rebuild's placement, the held one, the drawn one); `rebuild.ts` places each face sketch from its face on the built body (`placeSketch`: followed, or held with plane lost or ambiguous; greyed while its face's feature is off, Q3), builds every extrude off XY in its frame (`placedSpec`, with `flip`) with "source lost" from a held sketch, and `settleKept` keeps where a lost sketch lay (`KeptShape.placement`) and holds or forgets face placements (judged with nothing suppressed); `extrude.ts` Flip (`setExtrudeFlip`, `isFlipped`; the Extrude and Edit feature panels' tick box); `delete-ask.ts` says a sketch on a deleted feature's face stays with its plane lost. Tested in `test/face-sketch.test.ts` and, against the naming model on the real kernel, `packages/testing/journeys/naming.journey.ts`; in the browser in `apps/fission/e2e/extrude.mjs`.
- Step 26e, the timeline's rules on a face sketch: `document.ts` `greyedBy`/`greyedItems` (`walk`: an item built on a suppressed one, through its sketch or its sketch's face, is greyed naming that root, Q3; `builds` and `buildingItems` agree) and `rebuild.ts` gives an extrude of a suppressed sketch `greyed`, not failed; `timeline-bar.ts` items carry `greyed` (the root's name) and `warning` (`data-greyed`, dashed and faded; `data-warned`, red; `BarWiring.warnings`), Edit Sketch is off for a greyed sketch; `workspace.ts` `warningsOf` collects every plane, kept and source warning in timeline order for the panel, the bar and the browser's rows (`showKept`), and `showFailed` greys browser rows (`timeline-menu-greyed`); `sketch-tool.ts` `sketchDrawings` hides suppressed and greyed sketches and marks lost ones, which the viewport draws red over the body (`LOST_SKETCH_ORDER`); `timeline-edits.ts` refuses drops across a face link in words ("it is drawn on a face of Feature 1 (box)"); `delete-ask.ts` names what is built from a sketch that stays. Tested in `test/face-sketch.test.ts`, `packages/testing/journeys/face-timeline.journey.ts` (the app on the real kernel, five planted bugs) and `apps/fission/e2e/face-sketch.mjs`.
- Step 26d, picking where a sketch goes: `face-pick.ts` (`sketchSpot`: a pick while Create sketch offers the planes is an origin plane, a flat named face of a body shown (its name, feature and `drawnOnFace` frame), or refused with `CURVED_FACE` (Q5) or `UNNAMED_FACE` (a name two faces share); `sketchableFaces` the faces the viewport lights). `workspace.ts` `startSketch` offers all three origin planes (`ORIGIN_PLANES`, no longer "offered" one by one) and those faces, `pickFace` records `addFaceSketch` as "Create sketch" (a refusal goes in the panel's `#sketch-pick-error`), `lookAtSketch` passes the sketch's side (`sketch.ts` `sketchSide`; `sketchDrawings` gives `side` −1 on a back or bottom face) so the camera is outside the body. Tested in `test/face-pick.test.ts` (hand-worked box and cylinder), `test/face-pick-session.ts` (seeded scenes against a model with Rodrigues by angle), `test/face-pick-planted.test.ts` (ten plants), `packages/testing/journeys/face-pick.journey.ts` (real kernel) and `apps/fission/e2e/face-sketch.mjs` (browser).
- `timeline-edits.ts` (step 24e2): the timeline edits as pure document functions, the timeline model's rules: `deleteItem` (only that item; a sketch's content, a preset's profile no other item uses and an extrude's kept shape go with it; a marker on it moves to the item before, or "start"; the extrude of a deleted sketch stays and keeps its last shape through `settleKept`), `reorderItem` (to a place in the new order; the same document at its own place; refused with `before-dependency` or `after-dependent` and the app's message, "Extrude 1 can't go before Sketch 1: it is built from Sketch 1"), `suppressItem`, `moveMarker` ("start", an id, or null for everything); each one undo step named in `TIMELINE_STEPS` ("Delete Sketch 1"), `itemName` the short name. Hand-worked in `test/timeline-edits.test.ts`; on the real kernel and in the seeded sessions in packages/testing (`journeys/timeline.journey.ts`, `src/timeline-design.ts`). The app's buttons for them come with 24e4 and 24f.
- `timeline-bar.ts`: the timeline bar along the bottom (sketch feel SF3d: `timelineBarItems`, numbered items with suppressed, rolled-back and, from 24f2, `failed` flags; `showTimelineBar` draws them) and its logic (step 24f2): `dropIndex` and `tapPlace` (pointer x, item edges and the row's scroll to a place), `isDrag` (`DRAG_PIXELS`), `stepTarget` (the four playback buttons), `itemMenu` (entries and why one is off), `barEdit` (an action turned into the 24e edit and its undo step's name, a delete that says whether to ask, or the refusal's message). From 25a3 a sketch's menu leads with Edit Sketch (`edit-sketch`, `barEdit` answering `{ kind: "sketch" }`; off for the sketch already open, a suppressed one and one after the marker), and a double-click on an item (`isDoubleClick`, `DOUBLE_CLICK_MS` 500, the bar keeping its last click since a click redraws it; `BarWiring.onOpen`) opens it. Hand-worked in `test/timeline-bar.test.ts`, with seeded sessions against a plain model (`test/timeline-bar-session.ts`) and planted bugs (`test/timeline-bar-planted.test.ts`). From 24f3 `showTimelineBar` takes `BarWiring`: the rebuild's `failed` ids (red, `data-failed`), a right-click opens the item's menu, a click or tap picks it and shows its ⋯ (`data-more`) beside it; styles `src/styles/timeline-bar.css`. From 24f5 the bar is a row (`.timeline-marker`, styles `src/styles/timeline-marker.css`) of the four playback buttons (`playbackButtons`: each off with why when the marker would stay put or the sketch being edited would roll back; `data-button`) and the items; the marker is dragged (`barEdges` measures the items, shown where it would drop with the items after it greyed, a press under `DRAG_PIXELS` is no drag, the click after a drag is dropped) and a tap between items on a touch screen moves it (`tapPlace`; gaps 12 px at phone width); every one goes to `BarWiring.onAction`, and the bar draws itself again when nothing was recorded. - `timeline-reorder.ts` (step 24f6): an item dragged along the bar to a new place: `dragItem` (a press that moves `DRAG_PIXELS` drags it, dimmed, with a line where it would drop, `gapX`; the right button, a small press and a finger on an item that isn't picked drag nothing; the click after the drag is dropped, `swallowClick` in `timeline-bar.ts`), `dropState` (`barEdit`'s answer as the drag shows it: stays, moves, or refused with its words, shown above the bar); the drop goes to `BarWiring.onAction` as an `item` action. - `timeline-menu.ts` (step 24f3): the item menu wired, on the bar, the browser's Timeline rows and the Sketches rows: `menuEntries` (`itemMenu` with the entries that would leave no body off, `NO_BODY`, `leavesNoBody`; until the app shows an empty design) and `chooseEntry` through a `MenuHost` (`workspace.ts` `menuHost`, `openMenu`, `openRowMenu`): one `record` per edit with its `TIMELINE_STEPS` name, a delete recording `planDelete`'s settled document at once or after the box asks; `chooseBarAction` (24f5) does the same for a playback button, a marker drop or a tap (recorded, refused with its words, or nothing). 25a3: `MenuHost.editSketch` opens a sketch again (`workspace.ts` `openSketch`: out of any other sketch, panel or Extrude first, looked at, the kept warning hidden while it is open), `openAction` names the entry a double-click runs (on the bar, the browser's Timeline and Sketches rows); drawing rebuilds nothing until the sketch is left (`leaveSketch` rebuilds when the design changed since it was last shown, `shownDoc`), so the kept-shape warning comes after Finish Sketch.
