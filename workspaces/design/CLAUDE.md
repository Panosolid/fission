# Design workspace

Contents of `workspaces/design`; the rules are in packages/CLAUDE.md and the root CLAUDE.md. Keep this list current when you add or change a file here.

- the CAD workspace (`workspace.ts`, which names each undo step: "Change width" merged per size box, "Change shape to …"; a mount by Undo or Redo keeps the camera and keeps whatever in the selection still resolves by name on the body it rebuilt (`lastNames`); `profiles.ts` ready-made outlines until the sketcher).
- `commands.ts`: Design's commands as data (`DESIGN_COMMANDS`: `set_part`, `export_stl`), used by the toolbar and the AI assistant alike; `set_part`'s depth and height are optional (a left-out size keeps the current one in `run`; `documentFor` refuses one the shape needs), tested in `test/commands.test.ts`.
- `document.ts`: Design's data, version 2 (step 21b; docs/document-model.md, docs/project-format.md): `nextId`, profiles, sketches (curves: lines, circles round a point id from step 22b, arcs from a start to an end point id counter-clockwise round a centre point id from step 23a; `curvePoints`) and the timeline; `lastFeature` is the part's one solid feature (the last item that is not a sketch); `withPart` replaces it and keeps the sketches; `readDesignDocument` migrates version 1 (`migrateDesignV1`) and checks every sketch rule; `shown` names a file's value in a message without running its own `toString`.
- `sketch.ts`: sketch edits as pure document functions (`addSketch`, `drawLine` with ends as a point id to share or new coordinates, `drawRectangle` (step 22a: four chained lines, corners on existing points shared; `pointAt`), `drawCircle` (step 22b: round a point id to share or new coordinates, radius above 0), `drawArc` (step 23a: start, end and centre each a point id or new coordinates, ids taken in that order; one undo step "Draw arc"), `deleteCurve` dropping points only it used, `movePoint`), each one undo step named in `SKETCH_STEPS`; ids from the counter, never reused; `planeOf` the origin planes' frames; `sketchOutline` asks the kernel (`sketch_outline` with `sketchCurves`, the lines, circles and arcs apart, through `askOutline`) each time, never stored; `sketchLines` is the lines alone. Sketches are `design.sketch` objects with the generic `outlineProducer` (`representations.ts`, `designObjects(doc, kernel)`), timeline rows and rows of the browser's Sketches group ("sketches/<id>").
- `sketch-tool.ts`: the sketch tools as pure functions: `SKETCH_TOOLS` (Line, Rectangle, Circle, Arc, Tangent arc with keys L, R, C, A and T, steps 22a, 22c, 23b and 23c), `startTool`, `toolBusy` (what Escape ends), `clickAt` (line: a click at a kernel-snapped point draws a chained line sharing its start, landing on an existing point ends the chain; rectangle: first corner, then the opposite one, refused with a `note` on its row or column; circle: the centre (an existing point shared), then a point on the circle, the radius `distance` between them, refused with a `note` on the centre; arc: start, end, then a point on it, through `arcThrough` (step 23b: the centre on the line midway between the ends, ends swapped so it runs counter-clockwise, `null` within a rounding bound of one line), refused with a `note` for no end or no bend; tangent arc (step 23c): a free end (`leaving`: the way out where exactly one line or arc ends), then the arc's end, through `tangentArc` (the centre on the normal at the start, k = |w|²/(2·t × w)), chaining from its own end, refused with a `note` for no free end, no end or no turn; `bend` is the rounding bound both arcs share), `switchTool` (a tool switch: the line chain and the tangent arc hand over their last point), `drawStep` (the undo step's name), `previewLine`, `previewSegments` (what a click would draw), `circleSegments` (a circle as `CIRCLE_SEGMENTS` = 64 segments, drawing only), `arcSegments` (step 23a: an arc in ⌈64 × turn / 2π⌉ segments from its start to its end exactly), `sketchDrawings` (what the viewport draws: lines, then circles, then arcs, as segments), `curveCount` (the status's "4 lines and 1 circle", "2 lines, 1 arc and 1 circle"), `ORIGIN_PLANES` (only XY offered until 26), `PLANE_VIEWS`. `sketch-toolbar.ts` (sketch feel SF3b): the sketch's own toolbar, standing in for the modelling buttons (`data-modelling`) while a sketch is edited, Extrude kept: a Create menu (`createMenuRows`, each tool with its key; arrows by `menuMove`, Escape closes only the menu), the tool buttons (`sketch-tool-<kind>`, the one in use pressed) and a big Finish Sketch at the far right (`finishSketch` in `workspace.ts`: back to modelling with the camera at the home view, also from the panel's Finish sketch); styles `src/styles/sketchbar.css`; tested in `test/sketch-toolbar.test.ts`. `sketch-panel.ts`: the panel at the top of the properties (Pick a plane, then the tool's help, `TOOL_HELP` and `SNAP_HELP`, grid, Snap to grid (`DEFAULT_GRID_SNAP` off, in `sketch-tool.ts`; `workspace.ts` keeps the choice for every sketch until reload), Look at (SF2: `viewport.lookAt` on the sketch's plane, also on picking a plane), outline status, Finish sketch); styles in `src/styles/sketch.css`. `workspace.ts` runs the mode: Create sketch button, plane pick, `applySketchEdit` (one undo step per edit), Escape ends a chain or drops a first corner, centre or arc ends, `chooseTool` (buttons, L, R, C, A and T, through `switchTool`; a click the kernel was still snapping across a tool change is dropped), `editingSketch` keeps you in the sketch, with its tool, through Undo and Redo.
- `snap.ts`: where a sketch click lands (before constraints): after the kernel rounds the pointer (`rounding`, sketch feel SF1: `place_freely` to within half a pixel with grid snap off, the default; `snap_to_grid` with it on, or when the pixel size is unknown), within `SNAP_PIXELS` (10 px, turned to mm by `SketchPointer.pixel`) the nearest sketch point or line midpoint exactly, else lined up with the nearest point's column and/or row (H/V from the chain's last point), else the grid; never one of the tool's own clicks (`ownClicks`: a chain's last point, a first corner, a centre, an arc's start and end, a tangent arc's start), never the rectangle's first corner's row or column (`snapTargets`, `snapPointer`, `snapOnSketch`); `snapMarks` (square, triangle, ring, dashed guides sized in pixels), `snapLabel` ("endpoint 10,0", read by the e2e test), `withSnap` adds them to the edited drawing. `workspace.ts` snaps every hover and click through it.
- `extrude.ts`: extruding a sketch (step 21d): `extrudeSketch` (a new feature after the sketch, depending on it, replacing the part's solid feature until step 24; undo step `extrudeStep`), `setExtrudeDistance` (`DISTANCE_STEP`), `featureSpec` (the BodySpec of any feature; an extrude of a sketch asks the kernel for the outline at every build, so sketch edits rebuild it). `partOf` names it `sketch-extrude`; the panel shows only its distance (the height box). `extrude-panel.ts`: the Extrude panel (sketch, typeable distance, the kernel's closed/open answer, OK off until closed, Cancel and Escape); styles `src/styles/extrude.css`. `workspace.ts` opens it from the toolbar's Sketch group and the sketch panel (which finishes the sketch first), builds before recording, and rebuilds the part after an edit to the sketch it extrudes.
- `graph.ts`: the timeline in the project graph (`syncGraph`); every change and removal is recorded as a change note with `changeWords` ("you changed the box", "you removed the profile").
- `shortcuts.ts`: Design's keys in the one shortcut table (Escape clears the selection, Ctrl+C copies a selected profile as SVG, L, R, C, A and T choose sketch tools while a sketch is edited, `sketchToolKey`) and `designShortcut`, what each does for a selection; `workspace.ts` runs them in `shortcut`.
- `actions.ts`: Design's verbs: Download STL for a body, Copy as SVG for a profile (through the representation registry, then core's `outlineToSvg`; also Ctrl+C with one profile selected, through `shortcuts.ts`). `naming.ts` (step 24c): stable names for a body's faces and edges (`nameBody`, `featureNames`, `outlineCurves`): a face by feature, role and, for a side, the sketch curve ("face:feature-7/side:line-4"), an edge by the two faces it joins (the kernel's `edge_faces`; "A>B" between two sides, else the sorted "A+B"), ids escaped, any shared name dropped. `references.ts`: `bodyReference`, `designReference` (a face or edge by its name, null when it has none), `resolveReference` (the name back to a kernel index on the body now, null when gone), `readDesignReference`; `profileReference` ("outline:profile-1") for a profile, selectable from the browser's Profiles group (`browser.ts`); `selection.ts` labels each item ("Top face", "Edge of Front and Top"), lights faces and edges by name (`highlightOf`), keeps what still resolves after an edit (`survivors`) and says what it dropped (`droppedNote`), a profile too when the part has none. `test/views.ts`: a box's and a cylinder's `BodyView` written out by hand from primitives.rs.
- `test/free-placement.test.ts`: which kernel call rounds a pointer (`rounding`), grid snap off by default, the tools' help no longer promising the grid (SF1).
